Our Story

Where Earth Meets Art

Terra & Clay was born from a simple belief: that the objects we use every day should be as beautiful as they are functional. Working from our sun-filled studio in the countryside, we shape each piece by hand — from the first touch of raw clay to the final glaze firing.

We source our materials locally, fire with natural gas, and package in recycled paper. Every imperfection tells a story, and every piece is one of a kind.
